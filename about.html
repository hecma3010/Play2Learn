About Us

Play2Learn is a subsidiary of Kiskeya Studios responsible for developing children's cognitive games, such as Anagram Hunt and Math Facts Practice. Our focus is to make learning games more fun and engaging for children. This supports their cognitive development and stems from our desire for their mental growth to be healthy for the future.

This project began with the idea, "What if we make math or puzzle games fun?" This simple idea led us to the development of Play2Learn as an intuitive learning platform with a fun reward system for children.
