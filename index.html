Anagram Hunt

Anagram Hunt is a puzzle-solving game focused on finding anagrams. The game is based on the premise of discovering valid hidden words that can be formed using the exact letters of a given prompt word, such as "ARREST". Players have the option to choose their difficulty based on word length (5, 6, 7, 8, or 9+ letters) and must find as many anagrams as possible within a 30-second time limit.

Math Facts Practice

Math Facts Practice is a mathematical game focused on solving basic arithmetic problems (e.g., 5 + 6 = 11). The player must solve problems (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division) within a 30-second time limit. Each correct answer will add one point to their final score.
